Growth Capital

Capital and counsel for ambitious businesses ready to reach the next stage.

Start a Conversation How we invest

Overview

Fuel for the next stage of growth.

We empower ambitious businesses to reach new heights through a strategic blend of financial support, strategic guidance and industry expertise. Growth capital is for companies that have proven their model and now need the resources to scale it: entering new markets, developing new products, investing in people and infrastructure, or making a strategic acquisition.

Our investments are typically minority positions structured to fit the plan rather than the other way round. We are not looking to take over a business that is already working. We are looking to back the people running it, with capital sized to the opportunity and terms that keep everyone pulling in the same direction.

With a focus on custom-tailored solutions and risk management, we are a trusted partner on the journey to growth. That partnership continues long after the funds arrive.

What we look for

Partners with momentum.

Sector and geography matter less than the quality of the business and the clarity of its plan.

  • 01

    A proven model

    Established revenue, a clear customer base and unit economics that hold up under scrutiny. We back businesses that work and need room to grow.

  • 02

    A credible team

    Ambitious, capable management with a track record of delivery and the self-awareness to know where additional expertise would help.

  • 03

    A defined use of capital

    A specific plan for the funds, with milestones we can agree together and measure honestly along the way.

  • 04

    Room to grow

    A market large enough, or a position strong enough, that the next stage of growth is a matter of execution rather than hope.

How we invest

From first conversation to funded plan.

  1. 01

    Introduction

    A conversation about your business, your ambitions and what the next stage requires. No pitch deck is needed to start.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Focused diligence on the business, the market and the plan, drawing on our sector expertise. We share our thinking as we go.

  3. 03

    Structure

    A tailored proposal: the size of the investment, the instrument, the terms and the governance that follows. Designed for the plan, not a template.

  4. 04

    Investment

    Capital is deployed against the agreed plan, with milestones that keep the partnership honest and the business focused.

  5. 05

    Partnership

    Ongoing strategic guidance, access to our network of operators and advisers, and the option of follow-on capital as the business earns it.

Beyond capital

What comes with the investment.

  • 01

    Strategic guidance

    A sounding board for the decisions that shape the next stage: pricing, expansion, hiring, acquisitions and when to say no.

  • 02

    Industry expertise

    In-depth knowledge of specific sectors that helps management navigate sector-specific challenges and seize opportunities.

  • 03

    Connections

    Introductions to customers, senior operators, advisers and co-investors who can accelerate the plan.
