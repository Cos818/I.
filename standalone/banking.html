Services

What BOF Pay offers

Everything you need to hold, move and spend money, managed from the BOF BANK app or online.

01

Multi-currency accounts

Hold, send and receive money in major currencies from a single account, with local account details for incoming payments.

02

Cards

Physical and virtual debit cards linked to your account, with spending limits, instant freeze and real-time notifications.

03

Payments and transfers

Local and international transfers, scheduled and recurring payments, and bill payments from the app.

04

Foreign exchange

Convert between currencies at live rates, with the amount you will receive shown before you confirm.

05

Business accounts

Accounts for companies with team cards, expense controls, bulk payouts and statements for your accountant.

06

Security

Biometric login, two-factor authentication and instant alerts on every transaction keep your money protected.
