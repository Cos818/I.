# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):

- Marketing and brand decision-makers at established companies, so far in financial services, mining, professional services and chemicals. They arrive needing a rebrand, a credible digital presence, or campaigns that produce pipeline, often with compliance constraints.
- Founders and owners of owner-run businesses, so far in wellness, who need their first real brand, website, sales and marketing strategy and launch support.

Both are evaluating whether to start a conversation. The site's job is to make that decision easy and to route it to one email.

## Product Purpose

BBG Media is a marketing agency that builds brands that make commercial sense. It delivers brand identity, go-to-market, websites, paid campaigns, social media, video, creative and artwork, email and newsletters, and blog and SEO content. Success for the site is a qualified enquiry from either audience.

## Positioning

Brand and performance in one place (confirmed): identity, content and paid campaigns are run by the same hands, so brand and pipeline are built together rather than by separate suppliers. Supporting facts from existing copy: six years across financial services, mining, professional services, chemicals and wellness; the operator is a corporate marketer, brand builder, scaler and executor; clients are chosen as much as they choose, and every identity, campaign and asset must earn its keep or it does not ship.

## Operating Context

- Work spans regulated and industrial sectors where creative must stay compliant (noted on the brokerage case: compliant creative across Meta and LinkedIn).
- Paid media runs on Meta and LinkedIn. Websites are built to be found by people and answer engines (SEO and AEO) and to convert.
- Full case walkthroughs are reserved for consultation and are reference-checkable on request.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page static site, one HTML file with inline CSS and JavaScript, no framework or build step. Deployable to any static host.
- Contact is a mailto link to `hello@bbgmedia.com`. Undecided: whether this is the live inbox. Treat it as the working address, not a verified fact.
- Case studies are currently presented anonymously by industry. Undecided: whether names, logos or screenshots may ever be shown. Do not add them without approval.
- Social profiles: none confirmed. Placeholder links were removed; do not reintroduce links without real URLs.
- Typography: SF Pro Display is specified first in the font stack and renders on Apple devices; Inter is the open fallback elsewhere. SF Pro cannot be embedded under Apple's licence.
- The previous trading name must never appear on the site; the brand is BBG Media only.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: BBG Media. Logo supplied as a square JPG on black with chrome-outlined letterforms; crops live in `assets/` (`logo-nav.jpg`, `logo-lockup.jpg`, `logo-full.jpg`). Use the actual logo, never a typed substitute, and preserve its proportions.
- A second mark (liquid chrome blob with boxed BB/BG monogram) was supplied as reference for colour only; it is not placed on the site. Palette sampled from it: silver-lilac highlight #edddf8, lilac #9978bf, magenta #d30df6, electric blue #0385ff, deep violet #5b2d79 on black. Used as light and accent only.
- Voice: direct, commercial, lightly playful. The "Finance Barbie" reference is a deliberate part of the personality and stays (confirmed).
- Copy conventions in force: no em or en dashes; no invented clients, testimonials, awards, results or team members.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five anonymous case stories with industry, title and one-paragraph description (mining services group; two regulated brokerages; tax advisory firm; raw chemicals supplier; wellness and founders).
- Three figures: 6+ years of craft, 7+ brands grown, 6+ corporates rebuilt.
- Nine service descriptions.
- Absent and not to be fabricated: client names, project imagery or screenshots, testimonials, performance numbers, team bios, pricing, office location.

## Surface Notes

- Landing page motion vocabulary in force: inline chrome pill in the headline, one kinetic service strip, horizontal accordion for the five cases, scroll-scrubbed voice paragraph, magnetic CTAs, pointer spotlight on contact. All collapse to static under reduced motion.

## Product Principles

1. Every element must earn its keep: if it does not help a visitor decide to make contact, it does not ship.
2. Brand and performance are one offer; never present creative and campaigns as separate departments.
3. Credibility over claims: show real, reference-checkable work and real figures, say plainly what is withheld and why.
4. Speak to corporates and founders with the same voice; neither audience is an afterthought.
5. Presence is the entry fee: the site itself must demonstrate the craft it sells.
