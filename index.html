Ask about a corridor

Where routing complexity shows.

A good SWIFT route stays as it is. Slow or expensive correspondent chains are routed another way.

Correspondent chain today

  1. Your bank
  2. Correspondent 1
  3. Correspondent 2
  4. Local bank
  5. Beneficiary
  • Each hop adds time, fees and a separate FX deduction.
  • Tracking ends at the last correspondent that supports GPI.

The same payment with Toniva

  1. Your account
  2. Toniva
  3. Beneficiary
  • SWIFT or a local rail, chosen per corridor.
  • FX priced all-in, with a quoted settlement window.
  • One reference from origin to landed funds.
Typical todayWith Toniva
RailSWIFT by default, or one fixed correspondentSWIFT or local rails, per corridor
SettlementDepends on the route and correspondentA priced settlement window
FXSeparate, often marked upPriced per corridor, all-in
TrackingGPI where supported, then per correspondentOne reference, end to end
