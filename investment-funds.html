Investment Funds

Investment funds pool investors' monies together. Each BOF Fund is designed with different risk objectives and investment policies, which dictate the asset allocation within the fund, so you can choose the one that meets your specific needs.

Ask about BOF Fund 1Why invest through a fund

The BOF Fund 1

Through our BOF Fund 1, you can invest in specialist and geographic areas where you might not have the expertise and knowledge to invest on your own. By holding BOF Fund 1 units, you can invest in multiple asset classes including equities, bonds, commodities and alternative investments.

We use active asset allocation to take advantage of the changing opportunities in each asset class, to diversify risk and to seek to improve the returns on your investment.

How to invest in BOF Fund 1

Contact our Fund Management team at support@bofbank.com for further information on BOF Fund 1 and its subscription form.
