Private Equity

We invest in and partner with businesses to unlock their full potential.

Start a Conversation How it works

Overview

Active ownership, shared ambition.

We are not passive investors. When we take a significant stake in a business we work closely with management to identify and implement the strategies that enhance operations, increase efficiency and drive growth. The plan is built together and owned together.

Our approach suits established businesses with a defensible position and clear levers for improvement: a founder planning succession, a division that would thrive as an independent company, or a management team with a growth plan that needs a committed partner behind it.

Because we take a long-term perspective, we can invest in the things that create lasting value, from people and systems to new markets, rather than optimising for a quick exit.

Where we add value

Levers we pull alongside management.

  • 01

    Operational improvement

    Sharpening how the business runs: processes, systems, procurement, pricing and the metrics that show whether it is working.

  • 02

    Strategic repositioning

    Focusing the business on the markets and products where it can lead, and stepping back from those where it cannot.

  • 03

    Growth acceleration

    Investment in sales, product and geography, and support for acquisitions that strengthen the platform.

  • 04

    Governance and talent

    Building the board, the reporting and the leadership team a larger business needs, without losing what made it succeed.

How a partnership unfolds

From first meeting to lasting value.

  1. 01

    Origination

    An early, candid conversation about the business, its owners’ objectives and whether our approach is the right fit.

  2. 02

    Diligence

    A thorough, respectful assessment of the business, its market and its people, conducted with sector expertise and minimal disruption.

  3. 03

    Transaction

    A structure that reflects the objectives of everyone involved: owners, management and the business itself.

  4. 04

    Value creation

    An agreed plan for the first year and beyond, with priorities, resources and accountability shared between us and management.

  5. 05

    Long-term ownership

    Patient stewardship through the plan, and a realisation only when the business, and every stakeholder in it, is ready.

What we look for

Businesses with more to give.

  • 01

    A defensible position

    Established businesses with loyal customers, sound economics and a reason to exist that will outlast the current cycle.

  • 02

    Ambitious management

    Leaders who want a partner in the work of building, and who are candid about where help is needed.

  • 03

    Clear levers

    Identifiable opportunities to improve operations, extend the market or accelerate growth, where our engagement makes a difference.

  • 04

    A case for patient capital

    Succession, carve-outs and growth plans where the right outcome takes years, not months.
