Customised advice

Most investments carry risks that can feel too deep for your pockets. Customised wealth management advice helps you balance risk with reward so you can maintain stability despite fluctuations in the financial markets, without letting your other assets sink the boat.

Portfolios

Three ways to invest

01

Fixed Income Securities

Designed to generate positive total returns from a diversified portfolio of Sukuk, bonds, other fixed income securities and collective investment schemes. In normal circumstances the investment aims to deliver income in excess of the average rate achievable through a one-year cash deposit, including Islamic Capital Markets and Shariah compliant fixed income investing.

02

High Growth Securities

Appropriate for sophisticated investors seeking high growth. It can hold multiple asset classes but focuses on growth with less concern about volatility, so up to 100%* of net assets may be invested in international equity markets through regulated collective investment schemes, onshore and offshore.

03

Long-Term Securities

Appropriate for investors seeking growth over the longer term. The investment holds multiple asset classes, and up to 100%* of net assets may be invested in international debt and equity markets through regulated collective investment schemes, onshore and offshore.

* This percentage is indicative only and the Investment Manager may, if necessary, alter or adjust it because of prevailing market conditions.
